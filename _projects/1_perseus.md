Using Euclid's Early Release Observations of the Perseus cluster, I study the isophotal shapes of dwarf galaxies to identify tidally transformed systems. I develop a novel cumulative light-fraction approach for measuring the $$c_4$$ Fourier coefficient, designed for low-surface-brightness dwarfs. Combined with synthetic Euclid observations of N-body simulations, this work identifies the boxy dwarfs as tidally transformed remnants of moderately rotating progenitors.

Paper: _Euclid: Early Release Observations – The formation of peanutty dwarf galaxies in Perseus_, [A&A 714, A13 (2026)](https://doi.org/10.1051/0004-6361/202661021) ([arXiv:2605.21342](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.21342)).
