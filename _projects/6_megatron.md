Building on the [SKIRT](https://skirt.ugent.be) ionized-gas framework, I am applying the pipeline to cosmological hydrodynamic simulations to predict rest-frame optical emission from post-processed galaxy snapshots. The work uses MEGATRON zoom-in halos for direct comparison with JWST rest-optical spectroscopy at $$z \approx 4$$. A more self-consistent photoionization solver, coupled to the wavelength-resolved local radiation field, is being developed alongside this application.
