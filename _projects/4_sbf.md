Together with Reynier Peletier's group, I am building a two-band (VIS and NISP) surface brightness fluctuation pipeline for [Euclid](https://www.esa.int/Science_Exploration/Space_Science/Euclid) DR1 to measure distances to nearby dwarf galaxies in the semi-resolved regime. The pipeline anchors its blue calibration to TRGB distances and uses a twin-differential SBF measurement designed to work from the M81 group out to Fornax. The goal is to use SBF as a group-membership filter for building dwarf luminosity functions across the DR1 footprint. As old and intermediate-age stars brighten differently in the two bands, the pair of fluctuation magnitudes also constrains the dwarfs' star-formation histories, which I will test against the resolved-star histories of the M81 group dwarfs.
