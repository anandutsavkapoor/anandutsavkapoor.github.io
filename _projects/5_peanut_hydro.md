Following the observational identification of boxy, peanut-shaped dwarfs in Perseus (Kapoor et al. 2026), I am setting up simulations with [AREPO](https://arepo-code.org/) of how tidal interactions in a cluster environment transform disky dwarf progenitors into the peanut structures we observe. Gas-free tidal controls come first, with hydrodynamic runs to follow, and the suite will map how orbital and progenitor properties shape the observable $$c_4$$ signal.
